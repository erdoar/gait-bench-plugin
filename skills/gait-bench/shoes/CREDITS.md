# Library shoes: sources and credits

Every model here except `black-suede-platform-gold-16` comes from MakeHuman (makehumancommunity.org): the CC0 base mesh and its system shoes, and the community asset packs shoes01 (CC0), shoes02 and shoes03 (CC-BY). Each shoe was fitted to the same base-mesh foot through its `.mhclo` binding, cut to the right shoe, given per-vertex colours from its diffuse texture, and converted to `walkscene.shoe.v1`. Heavy meshes were simplified by merging vertices on a 1.5 to 5.5 mm grid. The MakeHuman system shoes had their modelled socks removed. The geometry is otherwise as the authors made it.

CC-BY models are used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The credit for each is its author and asset name below. CC0 models need no credit and are listed anyway.
